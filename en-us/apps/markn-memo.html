How it works

From passing thought to useful note.

Mark'n Memo uses the speech recognition available on your Android device. It saves the transcribed text—not an audio recording—so your thought is ready to read, organize, and act on.

1

Capture

Speak a quick note or enter it with the keyboard.

2

Organize

Put the note in a category that makes sense to you.

3

Remember

Set a reminder when a note needs to come back at a specific time.

Designed with restraint

No account. No cloud full of your notes.

Your memo text, category names, and reminder content stay on your device and are not sent to a Masatiki-managed server. Firebase Analytics helps us understand app usage, but note content is not included in those events.
