01

Before you begin

The app saves text

Mark'n Memo turns speech into text. It does not save an audio recording of your voice.

Memos are stored on your device

There is no account-based automatic sync. Use Data management in Settings to back up important memos regularly.

Speech recognition comes from your device

Supported languages, accuracy, and network requirements vary by device, Android version, and speech recognition service.

Review the terms before use

On first launch, you must agree to the Terms of Use and acknowledge the Privacy Policy. You can exit the app if you do not agree.

Important

Removing the app may also remove memos stored on the device. Create a backup before uninstalling the app, resetting the device, or moving to a new phone.

First hands-on experience

Try the hands-on guide with Markun

On first use, a small Markun guides you on Home after the short introduction. Read each speech bubble and tap the highlighted control yourself.

  1. Tap the large Markun on Home.If Android asks for microphone access, review and allow it to begin voice input.
  2. Say a few words, then tap the large Markun again.Your words become text and are saved in Temporary memos. If you press and hold instead, release to finish.
  3. After the saved message appears, tap the highlighted Temporary memos button.Read the memo you just made. The guide waits for you to finish checking it.
  4. Tap Try organizing.Tap the square beside your memo and check that it is selected.
  5. Tap the highlighted category folder button.Choose where to keep your memo. Ideas, Shopping, and Other are available on a fresh installation.
  6. Check that the memo has left Temporary memos.It moved to your chosen category; it was not deleted.
  7. Tap the treasure chest button.The category names appear.
  8. Tap the category you chose.Check that your memo is inside that category.
  9. Tap Try an alarm.Select the square beside the memo, then tap the highlighted clock button.
  10. Choose when to be reminded.This sets a real alarm. Check the clock mark and changed memo text color after saving.
  11. When you have checked the result, tap Next.Finish with Get started or open User Guide from the final message.

You can skip a step

Use Skip during voice input or Not now during organizing and alarms. Open Try voice input in Settings to try again. Memos created during the guide remain as ordinary memos. To remove a practice alarm, select the memo, tap the clock button, and choose Clear alarm.

Existing users do not automatically see the hands-on guide after updating. If you have deleted all custom categories, the guide skips organizing and continues to alarms.

03

Create a memo with your voice

Press and hold

  1. Press and hold the large microphone.Start speaking after the recording display appears and the phone gently vibrates.
  2. Say what you want to remember.You can pause briefly to think. Some devices may divide a long speech recognition session into parts.
  3. Lift your finger when you finish.The recognized text is saved as a temporary memo.

Tap to start and stop

  1. Tap the microphone once.The recording display and elapsed time appear.
  2. Say what you want to remember.
  3. Tap the microphone again to stop.The recognized text is saved as a temporary memo.

For better recognition

Hold the device about 8 to 16 inches (20 to 40 cm) away, speak in short sentences, and talk a little more slowly than usual. Move away from loud TVs, fans, or traffic.

Moving to another screen or turning off the display ends voice input for safety. Text already recognized is saved as a temporary memo when possible.

04

Type a memo

  1. Tap the input field at the bottom of Home.The keyboard appears.
  2. Type your memo.
  3. Tap Send.After the memo is saved, the field is cleared and the app returns Home.

When a category is open, use the plus button beside its name to add a memo directly to that category. After saving, the same category stays open so you can confirm the new memo.

05

Organize and read memos

A fresh installation starts with Ideas, Shopping, and Other in the app language. You can rename or delete these categories freely. Updating does not add them to existing categories or recreate deleted ones. Changing the app language does not automatically rename saved categories.

Move temporary memos to a category

  1. Open Temporary memos.
  2. Tap the square beside each memo.Tap All to select every memo shown.
  3. Tap the folder icon.
  4. Choose the category where you want to keep the memo.
  5. Check that the memo has left Temporary memos.It moved to the category; it was not deleted.
  6. Tap the treasure chest button at the top center.The category list opens.
  7. Tap the destination category name.
  8. Check that your memo is inside the category.

Create a category

  1. Open the category or chest screen.
  2. Tap the plus button at the top.
  3. Enter a category name and confirm it.

Switch without returning Home

Use the Temporary memos button at the top right of the category screen to open temporary memos directly. On the Temporary memos screen, use the chest button at the top center to return directly to categories.

Read, edit, and reorder

  • Tap memo text to edit it.
  • Select memos with the squares, then copy or delete them together.
  • Choose Newest, Oldest, or Custom order.
  • In Custom order, press and hold the three-line handle, then drag it up or down.
  • Deleted memos move to Deleted before they are permanently removed.

06

Set an alarm

  1. In Temporary memos or a category, select the square beside the memo you want a reminder for.
  2. Tap the clock button in the action bar.
  3. Choose a time.Select 5, 10, or 30 minutes, 1 hour, or choose a date and time.
  4. When choosing a date and time, tap OK.No second confirmation is needed.

Check that the alarm is set

A confirmation appears immediately after setting an alarm. The memo starts with a clock mark and its text becomes bold blue. The alarm time is not permanently shown in the memo list. To check or change the time, select the memo and tap the clock button. Choose Clear alarm to remove the alarm, its clock mark, and the alarm text color.

A notification can be late

Android power saving and the exact alarm permission can move the notification time. If the app shows instructions, check the permission in Android Settings.

Full-screen display while locked

On Android 14 or later, if full-screen alarm display is not allowed, the app explains this after you set an alarm. Use Open settings to allow it in Android Settings. If you do not allow it, the alarm still uses sound, vibration, and a high-priority notification.

You cannot save a time in the past. Future alarms are registered again after a device restart or app update.

07

Change settings

Language, theme, and background

Choose the app language, light or dark theme, background color, and casual or business appearance. In Casual mode, turn on Dress-up mode to let Markun's outfit and home-screen effects change with the seasons.

Special outfits and seasonal messages

Dress-up mode is off by default. Turn it on for Halloween outfits on October 24–31, Christmas outfits on December 18–25, and New Year outfits on January 1–7 each year. Markun returns to the usual seasonal outfit afterward.

For New Year, the Japanese app language shows a haori outfit; all other app languages show a shared celebration outfit. Changes follow your device's local date and work offline. Casual-mode messages also change during these periods, even when Dress-up mode is off.

Memo display

Show or hide creation dates and change the memo order.

App lock

Protect settings and memo screens with the device fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern, or password. Home stays available for quick capture.

Tutorial

Open Tutorial to review the short introduction. Choose Try voice input to create a memo by speaking, organize it into a category, and try setting an alarm with the hands-on guide.

App info

See the version, check for updates, open this guide, and read the privacy policy.

08

Backups and your data

Create a backup

  1. Open Data management from Settings.
  2. Choose Backup.
  3. Check the location and file name, then save.If Google Drive appears, Android's file picker can save there.

Restore a backup

  1. Choose Restore in Data management.
  2. Select a Mark'n Memo backup JSON file.
  3. Read the confirmation and continue.Restore replaces the current data with the backup contents.

Export and import

  • Export to TXT, JSON, CSV, or Android sharing.
  • Import supports compatible JSON created by Mark'n Memo.
  • Import adds data without deleting existing memos.
  • This is not automatic Google Drive sync. You choose a location for each operation.

Protect backup files

A backup contains memo text and settings. Check the destination and recipient, and do not store it where other people can read it.

09

Troubleshooting

Voice input does not start
  1. If asked for microphone access, choose Allow.
  2. Open Android Settings, Apps, Mark'n Memo, Permissions, and confirm that Microphone is allowed.
  3. Close other recording or calling apps and try again.
  4. Confirm that the device speech recognition service is enabled.
Speech is not converted correctly

Move to a quiet place, use short sentences, and speak a little more slowly. Also confirm that the app language matches the language you are speaking.

An alarm or notification does not appear
  1. Confirm that Android notifications are allowed for Mark'n Memo.
  2. Check that battery saving is not severely restricting the app.
  3. If exact alarm instructions appear, check the permission in Android Settings.
  4. Confirm that the device date, time, and time zone are correct.
I want to recover a deleted memo

Open Deleted in Settings. A memo more than 30 days past its deletion date is permanently removed during the next load and cannot be recovered.

Google Play update says it is unavailable

In-app updates do not work for an app installed outside Google Play. A Google Play installation and a newer eligible version are required.

A backup file cannot be imported

Confirm that it is compatible JSON created by Mark'n Memo. General TXT and CSV files cannot be imported directly. Keep the original file unchanged and retry with a copy.

This guide does not open

Check the internet connection and browser app. Save the PDF to the device if you need an offline copy.

Support

If the problem continues

When contacting support, include the device name, Android version, Mark'n Memo version, the action that caused the problem, and the exact message shown. Do not send private memo text.
